# Product strategy taxonomy

Classify before searching. Each returned product receives exactly one primary category and may receive secondary tags. `out_of_scope` is allowed only for irrelevant results.

| Category | Portfolio role | Selection basis | Sales role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `market_proven` | traffic / benchmark | task fit, mature supply, sales/stock/service snapshots | important signal |
| `novel_structure` | differentiation / content | uncommon structure, different operation, demonstrable problem solving | not an entry gate |
| `aesthetic` | click / brand / gifting | form, color, material appearance, style coherence, packaging | secondary signal |
| `scenario_specific` | conversion | explicit fit to one use scenario, audience, or task language | secondary signal |
| `premium_quality` | profit / reputation | material, thickness, workmanship, ergonomics, verifiable compliance | secondary signal |
| `solution_bundle` | AOV / cross-sell | coherent coverage of multiple task steps, not random bundling | secondary signal |

## Category rules

### Market proven

Use mainstream queries and compare relevant listings on sales, stock, MOQ, price band, services, and supplier repetition. Do not confuse high sales with high margin.

### Novel structure

Require visual review. Call a product novel only relative to the current retrieved sample unless external market evidence exists. Record the uncommon structural cue and the user problem it may solve. A cosmetic color change is not structural novelty.

### Aesthetic

Require visual review. Record visible style, color/material relationship, shape language, and content/gifting potential for the target audience. Never infer aesthetics from title alone.

### Scenario specific

Require explicit evidence that the product matches a source scenario, audience, or search phrase. Generic category relevance is insufficient.

### Premium quality

Treat material, food-contact, thickness, durability, and certification language as listing claims until documents or samples verify them. Premium price alone is not quality.

### Solution bundle

Map every component to a job step. Exclude random sets whose components do not form a coherent solution.

## Portfolio rule

Rank within categories, never with one universal score. A final portfolio may contain different winners for traffic, profit, content, conversion, brand, and AOV. If a category has no defensible candidate, report a category gap rather than filling it with a mainstream product.
